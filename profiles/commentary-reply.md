# Commentary-reply profile

**Audience:** readers of the original paper and adjacent specialists; the original authors.
**Voice:** direct, respectful, evidence-grounded. Disagreement is fine; ad hominem is not.
**Structure discipline:** state the original claim, state the disagreement (or extension), present the evidence, conclude. 2000 words is enough — anything longer suggests a separate paper.
**Banned patterns:** rehashing the original's arguments at length; "the authors fail to consider" without a citation to the omitted consideration; concluding by demanding retraction without procedural grounds.

## Drafter notes

No methods of our own (methods-writer, data-curator inactive). Figures are typically reanalyses of the original's data or simple counter-illustrations. peer-reviewer stays active to stress-test the rebuttal logic.
