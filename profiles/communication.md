# Communication profile

**Audience:** broad chemistry / chem-bio audience. The headline result must register in the first paragraph.
**Voice:** punchy, present-tense, opinion-restrained.
**Structure discipline:** lead with the result, not the motivation. The opening sentence carries the punchline. Background appears in service of the result, never the reverse.
**Banned patterns:** wind-up paragraphs ("Over the past decade, X has been …"); paragraph-long methods recaps inside the body; cumulative summary endings.

## Drafter notes

3500-word cap forces ruthless trimming. Methods section is brief; depth lives in Supplementary. Abstract is unstructured (single paragraph, ~150 words) because Communications privilege a single take-home over a multi-part summary.

A Communication that buries its result by the second paragraph is broken — restructure rather than polish.
