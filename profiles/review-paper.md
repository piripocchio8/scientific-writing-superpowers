# Review-paper profile

**Audience:** specialists and newcomers to the subfield; PhD students orienting themselves.
**Voice:** comprehensive, even-handed, but not without a point of view in synthesis sections.
**Structure discipline:** each body sub-section reviews one coherent strand; cross-strand synthesis lives in Outlook. Citations should be representative, not exhaustive — the goal is coverage of the conceptual landscape, not every paper ever written.
**Banned patterns:** chronological-only organization without conceptual grouping; tables that re-list what the text already says; "to the best of our knowledge" claims without a search method.

## Drafter notes

200-reference cap is firm but generous. Plot-maker stays active for schematic figures (concept diagrams, comparison tables-as-figures). No original data — methods-writer and data-curator inactive.
