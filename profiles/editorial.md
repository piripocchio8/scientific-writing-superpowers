# Editorial profile

**Audience:** the journal's readership broadly; community at-large.
**Voice:** personal, opinionated, conversational. First-person singular is allowed and often preferred.
**Structure discipline:** a single, clear thesis carried for 1500 words; one optional figure as visual anchor (typically a photograph or concept sketch); minimal references (≤20) as evidence anchors, not literature survey.
**Banned patterns:** abstract-style summary opening; "in this editorial we" meta-commentary; multi-part structure that fragments the argument.
