# Perspective profile

**Audience:** specialists who want a position, not a survey. Readers expect the author to commit to an argument.
**Voice:** opinion-led, present-tense, with explicit value judgments where the evidence supports them.
**Structure discipline:** every section advances the argument. A perspective that reads like a review with stronger adjectives is a failed perspective.
**Banned patterns:** "balanced" framing where the argument is real and one-sided; cumulative literature lists without commentary; the hedge "more work is needed" without specifying which work.

## Drafter notes

Outlook section (target 800 words) is where the perspective lives or dies — concrete predictions, falsifiable claims, named open problems. Vague "promising directions" don't count.
