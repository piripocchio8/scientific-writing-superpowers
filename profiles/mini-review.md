# Mini-review profile

**Audience:** specialists tracking a focused subfield. Shorter scope, same depth.
**Voice:** focused, declarative, opinion-restrained except in Outlook.
**Structure discipline:** one clearly bounded topic; no "and adjacent areas" creep. 100-reference cap forces selection over exhaustiveness.
**Banned patterns:** review-paper-style breadth in a mini-review-sized container; "due to space constraints" disclaimers — choose what matters and skip the rest.

## Drafter notes

5000-word total forces tight prose. Outlook is short (500 words); 1–2 concrete near-term directions beat 5 hand-waves.
